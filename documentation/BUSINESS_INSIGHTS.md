# Business Insights

## Purpose

This document summarizes the key business insights identified
from the Consumer & Product Analytics Platform.

---

## 1. Sales Performance

Sales performance is analyzed using total sales, order volume,
quantity sold, and sales trends over time.

Key areas include:

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Sales by Year
- Sales by Month
- Top Sales Orders

---

## 2. Product Performance

Product analysis identifies products and categories based on their
sales and profitability performance.

Key areas include:

- Top Products by Sales
- Top Products by Profit
- Category Performance
- Sub-Category Performance
- Loss-Making Products

---

## 3. Customer Performance

Customer analysis evaluates customer contribution to sales and
profitability.

Key areas include:

- Top Customers by Sales
- Top Customers by Profit
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Loss-Making Customers
- High-Sales and Low-Profit Customers

---

## 4. Regional Performance

Regional analysis compares business performance across geographic
areas and outlet types.

Key areas include:

- Sales by Region
- Profit by Region
- Sales by State
- Profit by State
- Sales by Outlet Type
- Loss-Making Regions

---

## 5. Discount and Profitability

Discount analysis evaluates the relationship between discounting
and profitability.

Key areas include:

- Profit Margin by Discount
- High-Discount Orders
- High-Discount Loss-Making Orders
- High-Margin Products
- Low-Margin Products

---

## 6. Advanced Analysis

Advanced analysis is used to identify performance relative to
overall business benchmarks.

Key areas include:

- Categories with the Highest Profit
- Sub-Categories with the Highest Profit
- Sales Above Overall Average
- Profit Above Overall Average
- Products Above Average Sales

---

## 7. Business Impact

The analysis provides a structured view of:

- Revenue performance
- Product contribution
- Customer contribution
- Regional performance
- Discount impact
- Profitability

Specific numerical findings should be added after the final
business queries are executed and verified.
