# Business Understanding

## 1. Business Scenario

The project analyzes retail business data to understand consumer,
product, sales, regional, discount, and profitability performance.

---

## 2. Business Problem

Management needs a clear analytical view of business performance
to understand which products, customers, and regions contribute
to sales and profitability.

---

## 3. Project Objective

The objective is to transform raw retail data into reliable
business insights that can support sales, product, customer,
regional, and profitability decisions.

---

## 4. Key Business Areas

### Sales

Understand sales performance and trends.

### Products

Identify high-performing and low-performing products.

### Customers

Identify valuable customers and customer contribution.

### Regions

Compare performance across geographic regions.

### Profitability

Understand profit performance and the relationship between
sales, costs, and discounts.

---

## 5. Initial KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Products
- Average Order Value
- Average Discount

---

## 6. Business Questions

### Sales

1. What is the total sales performance?
2. How does sales performance change over time?

### Products

3. Which products generate the highest sales?
4. Which products generate the highest profit?
5. Which products have weak profitability?

### Customers

6. Which customers contribute the most revenue?
7. How concentrated is revenue among customers?

### Regions

8. Which regions generate the most sales?
9. Which regions generate the most profit?

### Discounts

10. How does discounting affect profitability?
11. Which products or regions receive higher discounts?

---

## 7. Stakeholders

Potential stakeholders include:

- Business Management
- Sales Management
- Product Management
- Regional Management
- Finance
- Business Analysts

---

## 8. Scope

The project focuses on:

- Sales
- Customers
- Products
- Regions
- Discounts
- Profitability
- Business KPIs

---

## 9. Out of Scope

The following are currently outside the project scope:

- Machine Learning
- AI chatbot
- Recommendation engine
- Predictive forecasting
- Real-time analytics

These may be considered separately if a genuine business requirement
is identified.
